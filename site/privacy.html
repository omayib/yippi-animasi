Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what information Short-and-fun (the "Service"), operated by Arif Akbarul Huda in Yogyakarta, Indonesia, collects when you connect your TikTok account, how we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

When you log in with TikTok and approve the requested permissions, TikTok shares with us:

  • Basic account information: your TikTok open ID (an app-specific identifier), display name and avatar.
  • Access tokens: issued by TikTok so the Service can send videos to your drafts on your behalf.

We do not receive your TikTok password, phone number, email address, messages, followers list or viewing history. We do not use cookies for advertising or tracking.

2. How we use it

  • To identify your account and show you which account is connected.
  • To upload the videos you asked for to your TikTok inbox as drafts.
  • To keep the Service secure and fix problems.

We do not post to your profile, we do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

3. Sharing

Your information is shared only with TikTok, as needed to deliver videos through its API, and with infrastructure providers that host the Service under confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information if required by law.

4. Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted and are used only by the Service. We keep your information only while your account is connected. When you disconnect, or when a token expires and is not renewed, we delete the related tokens and account information within 30 days.

5. Your choices

  • Disconnect at any time: in the TikTok app, go to Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services, and remove Short-and-fun.
  • Request access or deletion: email us and we will respond within 30 days.

6. Children

The Service is intended for people who meet TikTok's minimum age requirement. Users under 18 should use it with a parent's or guardian's permission. If you believe a child has connected an account without permission, contact us and we will delete the information.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date above, and for important changes we will give notice through the Service or this page.

8. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: omayib@gmail.com
